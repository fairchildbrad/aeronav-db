- For each airport that cannot be tied to one cited rule, add `unplaced_by_design` with the specific missing fact, its `tz_unplaced` reason, and the statute/tzdata/FAA material searched. Do not infer a zone from latitude, longitude, an adjoining borough, or a Chart Supplement offset.

## AC-3: Arizona and Nevada (five scheduled open)
- Arizona: KFLG, KGCN and KPGA (Coconino); KSOW (Navajo). Resolve the Navajo Nation/Hopi or other local exception only with a cited jurisdiction or named-place rule that covers the airport. - Nevada: KEKO (Elko). Establish whether the cited Part 71 boundary or a sourced named-place rule covers this airport, then override or list it. Preserve `US-NV` as SPLIT; do not assign Elko County wholesale from a printed offset.

## AC-4: The three US Pacific islands
Resolve Palmyra (AP0019547), Wake (AP0019560), and Midway (AP0019610) by airport-keyed overrides applying a cited island/named-place rule. tzdata's `backward` links for Pacific/Wake and Pacific/Midway can identify candidate zones; confirm their applicability and history before using them. A `unplaced_by_design` entry alone cannot clear gate item 1, and a blank `source_conflict` override requires a real source conflict. If any island lacks sufficient cited placement, stop the closing gate and take the unresolved case to reviewers; do not add a reason code or silently exempt it. Record each disposition and its source in the SUMMARY.

## AC-5: The eight source conflicts and Chart Supplement check
- Revisit I20, 01L, 1V2, 4E8, 7Q7, M35, C64 and I45 individually. Retain the cited blank-tz override unless an independent applicable rule resolves the conflict; explain any change. These eight are not part of the 63 scheduled gate failures.
- Fetch the applicable FAA Chart Supplement edition during APPLY if needed; record edition/date, source URL and hash. Compare printed standard/DST offsets with candidate zones on suitable winter/summer dates. Record matches, mismatches, absent entries and edition caveats. The Chart Supplement checks a placement; it never supplies the rule that places an airport. Do not store copyrighted text.
