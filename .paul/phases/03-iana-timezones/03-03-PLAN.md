## AC-1: Gate clear for AU, BR, RU, ID
On a build from the seed, `tz gate` prints no line naming AU, BR, RU or ID. The SUMMARY gives, per country: placed, NULL by reason, scheduled placed, and scheduled in unplaced_by_design with reasons.

## AC-2: Partial list completes itself (load check)
The load also fails when a region of a multi-zone country is named by two or more zone1970.tab comments and isn't in partial_regions.csv. This adds RU-SA and RU-SAK, and anything else it finds, each with its tzdata quote. Tests cover it.

## AC-3: Australia
- **Sources:** each state or territory's standard time act, fetched and cited with its URL and sha256:
  - NSW Standard Time Act 1987: Broken Hill (Yancowinna county) on South Australian time; Lord Howe Island.
  - Qld Standard Time Act 1894; WA Standard Time Act 2005; Tas Standard Time Act 1895.
  - Vic Summer Time Act 1972 and its standard time provisions; SA Standard Time Act 2009; NT Standard Time Act 2005; ACT Standard Time and Summer Time Act 1972.
- **Region rules:** VIC → Melbourne, SA → Adelaide, NT → Darwin. ACT → Sydney (zone1970 doesn't name the ACT; the citation is the ACT act plus tzdata's Australia/Canberra link to Sydney in `backward`).
- **NSW:**
  - SPLIT. Broken Hill and the Yancowinna county places are name-cited → Broken_Hill. Lord Howe Island → Lord_Howe.
  - Everything else is the NSW act's residual (S2 boxes around the named places; a 0.5° margin at the SA border near Broken Hill).
- **QLD:**
  - SPLIT. Lindeman names only the Whitsunday Islands, and differs from Brisbane only in 1992–94.
  - Places named in the Whitsunday Islands → Lindeman. Every other place is the Qld act's residual → Brisbane, with an S2-style box around the Whitsundays.
- **WA:**
  - SPLIT. Eucla, and the Eyre Bird Observatory on Asia/Tokyo, are tzdata anomalies against the WA act (unofficial Central Western time). They're placed only if they meet L1; otherwise they go to unplaced_by_design.
  - The rest of WA is the WA act's residual → Perth, keeping 0.5° west of 125.5°E (tzdata gives the Eucla area's western edge as Caiguna) and outside any named place's box.
- **TAS:** Macquarie Island → Macquarie (no airport expected). Everything else → Hobart.
- **Canaries:** YSSY Sydney, YBBN Brisbane, YPPH Perth, YPAD Adelaide, YBHI Broken_Hill.

## AC-4: Brazil
- **Sources:** Decreto 2.784/1913 as amended by Lei 11.662/2008 and Lei 12.876/2013, from planalto.gov.br. Brazilian statutes are not copyrighted (Lei 9.610/98 art. 8 IV), so short quotes are fine.
- **Region rules:** one per whole state, with the zone from zone1970's state list, the law's zone and tzdata's zone history.
- **AM:** SPLIT. Lei 12.876/2013 lists the western Amazonas municipalities by name; those rows → Eirunepe, the rest → Manaus.
- **PA:** SPLIT. Belem and Santarem have been identical since 2013, when Lei 11.662/2008 put all of Pará on UTC-3. Santarem → Santarem name-cited, the rest → Belem, with the pre-2013 caveat (S4-style).
- **PE:** SPLIT. Fernando de Noronha → Noronha; mainland → Recife.
- **Canaries:** SBGR Sao_Paulo, SBEG Manaus, SBBE Belem, SBRB Rio_Branco, SBFN Noronha.

## AC-5: Russia
- **Source:** Federal Law 107-FZ "On the calculation of time" (2011, as amended), art. 5, which lists the subjects per zone and names the Sakha uluses. From pravo.gov.ru or publication.pravo.gov.ru. Official Russian acts are not copyrighted (Civil Code art. 1259 part 6).
- **Region rules:** one per whole subject, from the art. 5 list and the matching tzdata zone. Where tzdata keeps a separate zone for a subject (Kirov, Volgograd, Astrakhan, Saratov, Ulyanovsk, Barnaul, Tomsk, Novokuznetsk, Chita), the zone1970 comment names the subject.
- **SA (Sakha):** SPLIT. Ulus membership comes from art. 5, quoted in tzdata, plus a municipality-level gazetteer if a public-domain one exists. Otherwise only places tzdata names go in (Khandyga, Ust-Nera, Srednekolymsk), and the rest of Sakha's scheduled airports go to unplaced_by_design with the reason and what was searched.
- **SAK (Sakhalin):** SPLIT. North Kuril places → Srednekolymsk, Sakhalin Island places → Sakhalin, both name-cited.
- **Crimea is not in scope.** UA-43 is placed in 03-04 per Q1.
- **Canaries:** UUEE Moscow, UNNT Novosibirsk, UHWW Vladivostok, UEEE Yakutsk, UHPP Kamchatka.

## AC-6: Indonesia
- **Source:** Keputusan Presiden 41/1987, which lists provinces per zone (WIB/WITA/WIT).
- **Provinces created since 1987** inherit their parent's zone through the founding law that splits the province. Each row cites both laws.
  - Examples: Banten from West Java; Gorontalo from North Sulawesi; North Kalimantan from East Kalimantan; the 2022 Papua provinces from Papua and West Papua.
  - This is a new kind of membership (derived region), so reviewers rule on it as part of this plan.
- **Zone choice:** the WIB zones are Jakarta and Pontianak (identical since 1988). West and Central Kalimantan → Pontianak per zone1970 ("Borneo (west, central)"); every other WIB province → Jakarta.
- **Canaries:** WIII Jakarta, WADD Makassar, WAJJ Jayapura, WIOO Pontianak.
