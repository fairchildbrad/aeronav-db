<objective>
## Goal
Every present airport gets an IANA zone in `airport_identity.tz` with `tz_basis` saying which rule placed it, or tz NULL when the rules cannot place it. The columns already exist in schema 3 (NULL today), so no schema change.

## Purpose
cv-aviation converts logbook local times. Requirement (ROADMAP): "tz is never guessed. tz_basis 'country' is allowed only for single-zone countries. An airport in a multi-zone country that the region/override rules can't place gets tz NULL."

## Output
A rules table in the repo, a pure resolver, build wiring, verify checks, tests, and a coverage report. No polygons, no new dependency. Nothing is published.
</objective>

<acceptance_criteria>
## AC-1: Rules table
`identity/tz/rules.csv` columns `iso_country, region, county, tz, note`. region and county may be `*`. `identity/tz/overrides.csv` columns `airport_id, tz, source`, where source cites where the zone was read (e.g. the Chart Supplement page and edition). Both are plain CSV, sorted, and reviewed as data.

## AC-2: Resolution (most specific wins)
override → (country, region, county) → (country, region, `*`) → (country, `*`, `*`). tz_basis is `override`, `county`, `region` or `country` accordingly. If nothing matches, tz and tz_basis are NULL. Two rules at the same specificity that disagree are a load error, not a pick.

## AC-3: Never guessed
- A `country` rule (region `*`) is refused at load for any country with more than one zone in zone1970.tab.
- A `region` rule for a region that zone1970.tab or the rules themselves split by county is refused unless every county in that region with an airport is covered by a county rule or an override. Where this cannot be proven, the region rule is omitted and those airports stay NULL.
- Every tz is a canonical zone in zone1970.tab and belongs to that row's country there (a tz listed for a different country is refused).

## AC-4: Build and verify
The build fills tz/tz_basis for present identities. Absent identities keep their last value. `verify` checks: every non-NULL tz is in the host's tzdata; tz_basis is NULL exactly when tz is NULL; no `country` basis in a multi-zone country. It prints coverage: present airports with tz, NULL count by country (top 10). Canary: KPBI/KDJT resolves to America/New_York, PHNL to Pacific/Honolulu, KELP to America/Denver, PANC to America/Anchorage.

<!-- cv-aviation-added 2026-11-19 -->
## AC-7: Unplaced is visible
When tz is NULL, tz_basis is NULL too, and a new nullable column `airport_identity.tz_unplaced` gives the reason: `multi_zone_country`, `no_region`, `no_county_match` or `no_rule`. It is non-NULL exactly when tz is NULL. Schema 3 is unreleased, so the column goes into the DDL directly; cv-aviation gets the DDL delta. The manifest gains `tz: {placed, unplaced}` for present airports, and verify reports the change in unplaced since the previous file.

## AC-5: Chart Supplement validation (US)
For a sample of ≥ 5 airports in each US multi-zone state (FL, IN, KY, TN, TX, KS, NE, SD, ND, MI, ID, OR, AK), compare the resolved zone's standard UTC offset with the Chart Supplement's printed offset ("UTC-6(-5DT)"). Record the edition, airport and both values in the SUMMARY. Any mismatch is fixed in the rules before unify. If the Chart Supplement cannot be fetched or parsed, say so and do not claim AC-5.

## AC-6: Coverage report
SUMMARY reports: present airports, how many have tz by basis, NULL count by country, and every multi-zone country left wholly NULL.
</acceptance_criteria>
